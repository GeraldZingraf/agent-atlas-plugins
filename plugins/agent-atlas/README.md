# Agent Atlas

Map Your Month for business owners. Claude reads your last 30 days of email and calendar, read-only,
and writes you a private report: where your time went, dropped balls with replies drafted, the work
you repeat, where you're the bottleneck, five things worth taking off your plate, and draft
instructions for a Claude chief of staff.

## Use it

1. Connect Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365, in Claude under Customize > Connectors.
2. Start a new chat and say "map my month."
3. Answer three questions. Claude does the rest in about 10 minutes.

## Data

Your email and calendar never leave your Claude account. Nothing is sent, drafted, deleted, or
changed in your mailbox or calendar.

The plugin includes the Agent Atlas connector. It sends something to Agent Atlas only when you say
yes, and Claude shows you exactly what will be sent first:

- At the start, if you agree: your first name and business type.
- At the end, if you agree: the share-safe summary (categories, counts, hours, and five
  opportunities, with no names and no email content) and the email address you choose.

The connector refuses anything that looks like pasted email. You can skip both, or leave the
connector disconnected, and the scan works the same.
